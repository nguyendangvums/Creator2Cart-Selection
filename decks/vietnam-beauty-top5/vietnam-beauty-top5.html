A decade of trusted skincare picks and beauty favorites

Trinh Phạm has shared makeup, skincare and lifestyle videos since 2014, known for her yearly Best of Beauty roundups and budget-smart picks like skincare under 500K VND. Her warm, explanatory style has built 1.2M subscribers and 178M views across nearly 800 videos. A natural fit for skincare, cosmetics, haircare, mother-and-baby and lifestyle brands seeking credible, long-form endorsement.

Changmakeup

The lipstick expert who built her own beauty brand

Changmakeup made her name with detailed lipstick swatches and reviews, then launched her own cosmetics label, OFÉLIA, in 2016. She was the first Vietnamese female beauty blogger to earn YouTube's Gold Play Button, and her library of 238 videos has drawn 194M views. Ideal for lip and color cosmetics, beauty retail and founder-led brand storytelling.
